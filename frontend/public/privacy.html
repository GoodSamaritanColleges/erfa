eRFA Privacy Policy

Electronic Request for Approval · Good Samaritan Colleges · Effective November 9, 2026

eRFA is an internal system used by Good Samaritan Colleges to create, route, approve, and track institutional Requests for Approval (RFAs). This policy explains what information eRFA uses and how it is handled.

Who can use eRFA

Only people registered by an eRFA administrator can sign in. There is no public sign-up. Anyone who signs in with an unregistered Google account is refused access.

Information we receive from Google sign-in

When you sign in with Google, eRFA receives only your name, email address, and confirmation that your email is verified. eRFA does not receive your Google password, contacts, files, or any other Google data.

Information you provide in eRFA

  • Request details you enter on an RFA, such as title, purpose, department, and amounts.
  • Attachments you upload to an RFA.
  • Approval decisions and remarks entered by approvers.

How the information is used

  • To confirm your identity and permissions when you use eRFA.
  • To route RFAs to the correct approvers and show them the request.
  • To send email notifications about RFAs that need your action or have changed status.
  • To keep an audit trail of who did what and when, for institutional accountability.

Where it is stored

eRFA records are stored in Google Sheets and attachments in Google Drive, within Good Samaritan Colleges' Google Workspace. Your sign-in is kept only in your browser tab and is cleared when the tab is closed.

Who can see it

An RFA can be viewed only by the person who filed it, its assigned approvers, eRFA administrators, and authorized implementation or procurement staff once the RFA is approved. eRFA does not sell, rent, or share your information with anyone outside the institution, and does not use it for advertising.

Retention

RFA records and their audit history are kept as institutional records according to Good Samaritan Colleges' record-keeping policies.

Questions and requests

For questions about this policy or to request a correction to your information, contact the eRFA administrator at erfa@goodsam.edu.ph.
